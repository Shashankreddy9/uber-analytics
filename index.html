Turn the patterns into next steps
01 · Staff for the rush

02 · Separate volume from yield

More trips do not necessarily mean higher average fares or profit.

03 · Validate location names

Join pickup location IDs to the official TLC taxi-zone lookup before reporting neighborhood hotspots.

04 · Add context

Weather, events, airport traffic and service disruptions can help explain daily spikes.
